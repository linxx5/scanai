# Words the app shows — short and kind

## No internet
- "Offline. Your work is safe on this phone."
- "3 papers will send when internet comes back."
- "This needs internet. Queue it for later?"

## Free limit (5 scans)
- "Free: 5 scans. You used 5. Make an account to do more."
- "Your papers stay safe. Log in and pick up where you left off."

## Login wall (only when needed)
- "This needs an account: batch export, cloud AI, sync, share forms."
- Buttons: "Make account" / "Log in" / "Stay as guest"

## Errors (never lose papers)
- "Scan failed. Your photo is kept. Try again?"
- "1 paper failed. The rest are fine. Retry just this one?"
